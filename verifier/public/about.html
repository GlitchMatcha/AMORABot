How the 18+ check works

You prove one thing: "I am 18 or older". Nothing else about you is shared with AMORA.

Think of it like a stamp

Normally, to prove your age you show your whole ID: name, photo, birthday, address. With this check, your phone does the looking instead. It checks your ID privately and gives AMORA only a sealed "yes, 18 or older" answer. It's like a bouncer who gets a stamp that says 18+ without ever seeing your ID.

The clever maths behind this is called a zero-knowledge proof. It is simply a way to prove something is true without showing the information behind it.

What happens, step by step

  1. You install the free ZKPassport app and hold your passport or ID card against the back of your phone. It reads the small chip inside, the same way airport e-gates do.
  2. Your phone checks everything itself. It confirms the chip is genuine and works out your age, all on your phone.
  3. Your phone sends back only the answer: "18 or older: yes". Your passport details stay on your phone.

What AMORA can see

  • That you are 18 or older (yes or no).
  • Your Discord username and ID, so we know which account to unlock.
  • A scrambled code ("fingerprint") that stops one passport from unlocking lots of accounts. It looks like random letters and numbers.

What AMORA can never see

  • Your name, birthday or exact age (only that you are 18 or older).
  • Your photo.
  • Your nationality or address.
  • Your passport or ID number.
  • A copy of your ID. We never receive one, so there's nothing for us to lose or leak.

About "Log in with Discord"

We ask you to log in only to be sure the right account gets unlocked, so nobody can verify on your behalf. AMORA gets your Discord username and ID. We don't get your email, messages, friends or servers, and we can't post as you.

Honest limits

  • The ZKPassport app is made by a separate company. You trust it the way you trust any app. Their own page explains how it works: zkpassport.id.
  • The scrambled fingerprint can only be recreated by someone who already has your passport's data, for example the country that issued it. AMORA does not have that data.
  • You need a passport or ID card with a chip (look for a small chip or camera-like symbol on the cover). If yours isn't supported, ask the AMORA staff for help.
