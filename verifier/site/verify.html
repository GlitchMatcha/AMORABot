TL;DR for the "I will not read the long page" crowd:

  • Is my private information shared? No. Your passport or ID is read by the Self app on your own phone. AMORA only receives "18 or older: yes" and a scrambled code that stops one document from unlocking many accounts.
  • What does AMORA never get? Your name, birthday, photo, nationality, address, document number, or a copy of your ID.
  • Why can you trust it? You don't have to take Matcha's word for it, you trust maths. The proof is checked cryptographically, and since we never receive your ID there is nothing for us to leak, sell or lose.

Matcha can't leak your birthday. Matcha doesn't know it. We only get the "18+" wristband, same as the bouncer, minus the bad music.
