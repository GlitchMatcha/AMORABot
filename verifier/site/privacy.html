Privacy

This site is the age-verification portal of the AMORA Discord community.

What we receive

  • Your Discord user ID and username, from "Log in with Discord". Only the identify permission is requested: no email, messages, friends or servers.
  • The result of the zero-knowledge proof: only "18 or older".
  • A one-way, keyed fingerprint derived from your document's unique identifier. It stops one document from verifying several accounts and cannot be turned back into your document.

What we never receive

Your name, birthday, photo, nationality, address, document number or a copy of your ID. Your document is read by the Self app on your own phone.

Cookies and storage

This site sets no cookies. A short-lived login (15 minutes) is kept in your browser tab's session storage and is erased when you close the tab. No advertising or analytics, no trackers, no third-party scripts. The page talks only to the AMORA verification service and Discord (for login). Your phone's Self app sends the proof to AMORA through Self's relay service, which is covered by Self's privacy policy.

Who sees it

The verifier passes the result to the AMORA bot, which records that your Discord account is verified and updates your roles. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.

Questions or removal

Ask the AMORA staff in the Discord server.
