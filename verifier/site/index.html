AMORA age verification

This site belongs to the AMORA Discord community. It is used for one thing: letting members prove they are 18 or older to unlock the adult channels.

What this site never does

  • It never asks for your Discord password, token or email.
  • It never asks for payment details, phone numbers or card numbers.
  • It never receives a copy of your passport or ID.
  • It does not download or install anything on your device.

How you get here

You open this site only from a private (ephemeral) message sent to you by the AMORA bot after you run /verify or press the 18+ button in the server. The link is personal, single-use and expires after 15 minutes. If someone sent you this link in a DM or any other way, do not use it.
